Send Analytics Report

Generate and send a summary of the current ProfilePath analytics to the school principal or another authorized school official.

MMCL staff addresses only (@mcl.edu.ph). Type the address yourself; the recipient does not need a ProfilePath account.

Report covers

—

The school year and filters chosen at the top of this page. The latest data is used when the report is sent.

What the report includes

  • Assessment participation: students expected, registered, completed and not yet completed
  • Career Electives Worksheet completion and program matches at or above the similarity level
  • Assessment completion by section
  • Career interests: average RIASEC scores and the top 5 programs
  • Registered students by strand and completion by academic year

Sent as a PDF report attached to the email, with charts and tables. Totals and percentages only: no student names, Student Numbers or assessment answers.
