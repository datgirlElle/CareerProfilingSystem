About ProfilePath

Your career path starts with knowing your interests

ProfilePath is the career profiling system of the Center for Guidance and Counseling (CGC) of Mapúa Malayan Colleges Laguna. Senior High School students take a career interest assessment, and ProfilePath turns their answers into a profile of their interests, the careers that fit it and the MMCL programs worth considering.

How the CGC takes part

  • ✓Schedules your assessment and gives you the access code on the day.
  • ✓Reviews class results and follows up with students who may need extra support.
  • ✓Answers counseling and academic advising requests.
  • ✓Posts announcements and reminders to your account.

How it works

From sign-in to your results in four steps

1

Sign in

Use your Student Number. New here? Create your account with your MMCL student email.

2

Take the assessment

Get the access code from the CGC and answer the career interest questions in one sitting.

3

See your results

Get your Holland Code, your interest profile, and the careers and MMCL programs that fit you.

4

Talk to the CGC

Ask your guidance counselors for counseling or academic advising whenever you need it.
