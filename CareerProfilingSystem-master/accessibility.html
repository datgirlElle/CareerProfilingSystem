Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026 · ProfilePath Career Profiling System, Mapúa Malayan Colleges Laguna

Our Commitment

We want every student, counselor, and administrator to be able to use ProfilePath, regardless of ability or the assistive technology they rely on. Accessibility is something we actively build into the interface, not an afterthought.

What We've Built In

  • Skip-to-content links on every page, so keyboard users can bypass repeated navigation.
  • Keyboard support throughout — every modal, dropdown, and dialog can be closed with Escape, and interactive elements are reachable and operable by keyboard alone.
  • Labeled controls — icon-only buttons and placeholder-only inputs (search boxes, password fields, and more) carry proper aria-label attributes for screen readers, not just visual placeholder text.
  • Color contrast across the interface is checked against WCAG AA thresholds (4.5:1 for body text), including after visual redesigns.
  • Reduced motion support — every animation and transition in the app respects your operating system's "reduce motion" preference and is disabled automatically when it's set.
  • Live-region feedback on forms, so validation messages (like password-match status) are announced to screen readers as they change, not just shown visually.
  • Visible focus states on interactive elements, so keyboard navigation is easy to track visually.

Known Limitations

ProfilePath has not yet undergone a formal, independent WCAG 2.1 conformance audit. Some third-party visualizations (the Chart.js charts on the Analytics and Results pages) have limited built-in screen-reader support; the underlying numbers are always also shown as text nearby. We're continuing to improve accessibility as the system evolves.

Reporting an Issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere in ProfilePath, please let us know through the Help Center once signed in, or contact the Center for Guidance and Counseling directly. Tell us the page you were on and what assistive technology you were using, if any — it helps us fix it faster.
