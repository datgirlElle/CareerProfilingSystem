Terms of Use

Last updated: August 2026 · ProfilePath Career Profiling System, Mapúa Malayan Colleges Laguna

1. Who May Use ProfilePath

ProfilePath is provided exclusively for currently enrolled MMCL Senior High School students, and for Guidance Counselors and Administrators authorized by the school. Student registration is restricted to official @live.mcl.edu.ph email addresses and requires verifying that address before the account can be used.

2. Your Account

  • You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity under your account.
  • Each student may hold only one account, under their own name and LRN. Impersonating another student is not permitted.
  • Information you provide at registration must be accurate and kept up to date.

3. Nature of Career Recommendations

The RIASEC assessment and the career program recommendations it produces are guidance tools meant to support — not replace — your own judgment and a conversation with the Guidance Office. A high or low match score is not a guarantee of admission, aptitude, or success in any program. Always discuss your results with a Guidance Counselor before making decisions about your academic track.

4. Acceptable Use

When using ProfilePath, you agree not to:

  • Attempt to access another student's account, results, or messages;
  • Attempt to bypass, probe, or interfere with the system's security, encryption, or access controls;
  • Submit false information on the assessment, worksheet, or registration form;
  • Use the platform for any purpose other than your own career profiling and guidance.

5. Guidance Counselor & Administrator Accounts

Staff accounts are created directly by an Administrator and are subject to the same confidentiality obligations, plus the additional responsibility of accessing student data only as needed to perform their guidance or administrative duties, consistent with our Data Privacy Policy.

6. Account Suspension

The school may suspend or deactivate an account that violates these terms, is used for a purpose other than career profiling and guidance, or poses a security risk to other users' data.

7. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as the system evolves. Continued use of ProfilePath after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

8. Questions

If you have questions about these terms, reach out through the Help Center once signed in, or contact the Center for Guidance and Counseling directly.
