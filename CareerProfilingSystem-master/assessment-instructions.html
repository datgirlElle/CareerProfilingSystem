Assessment Instructions

What to expect before you begin the RIASEC Career Interest Assessment.

Before You Begin

This assessment is based on Holland's RIASEC theory and helps identify the careers and work environments that best match your interests. Please read the instructions below before you begin.

60 Statements

10 statements each for 6 interest areas: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional.

About 10–15 Minutes

Your progress is saved automatically, so you can pick up where you left off if you need to step away.

There Are No Right or Wrong Answers

Rate each statement honestly, from Dislike to Enjoy, based on your genuine interest — not what you think sounds best.

Unlocks Your Career Worksheet

Once you finish, you'll see your RIASEC results and be able to start the Career Worksheet.

Once you begin, you'll need to answer every question in order — you can't skip ahead or submit until all 60 are complete.
