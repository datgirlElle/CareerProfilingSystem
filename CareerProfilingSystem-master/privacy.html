Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · ProfilePath Career Profiling System, Mapúa Malayan Colleges Laguna

1. Introduction

This policy explains how ProfilePath collects, uses, protects, and retains personal information, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. By creating an account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity information: first and last name, LRN, email address, strand, section, and grade level, provided at registration.
  • Assessment data: your responses to the 60-item RIASEC interest inventory and the resulting Holland Code scores.
  • Career Worksheet data: the program you state an interest in and the electives you select.
  • Support communications: messages you send to the Guidance Office through the Help Center, including advising requests.
  • System activity: account and security-relevant actions (logins, registrations, password changes) recorded in the audit trail.

3. How We Protect Your Information

All of the personal and content data listed above — your name, program data, question text, help-request messages, and audit log detail — is encrypted at rest using a Modified AES-256 cipher before it is stored, and decrypted only at the moment an authorized request reads it. Passwords are never stored in a reversible form; they are hashed with bcrypt.

Access to your data is enforced server-side through role-based access control: only you, your assigned Guidance Counselor(s), and school Administrators can view it, and every access-relevant action is written to a permanent, tamper-evident audit trail.

4. Why We Collect It

Your information is used solely to operate the career profiling and guidance process: computing your RIASEC profile, generating career program recommendations, letting Guidance Counselors support you, and maintaining institution-wide analytics in aggregate, non-identifying form. It is never sold, rented, or used for advertising.

5. Who Can See It

  • You can always view your own profile, results, and worksheet.
  • Guidance Counselors can view student records, RIASEC results, and counseling requests to provide support.
  • Administrators have full oversight of the system, including security configuration and the audit log.
  • Other students never have access to your individual data.

6. Data Retention

Your account and assessment records are retained for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to support academic advising continuity. Audit log entries are retained according to a retention period set and reviewed by the Administrator.

7. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to be informed, to access your personal data, to request correction of inaccurate data, and to lodge a complaint with the school's Data Protection Officer. To exercise any of these rights, contact the Center for Guidance and Counseling (CGC) through the Help Center once signed in, or reach out to the CGC directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the system evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
